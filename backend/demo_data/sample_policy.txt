FINPILOT ENTERPRISE FINANCIAL OPERATIONS POLICY (SOP-2026-V1)

1. OVERVIEW & SCOPE
This document outlines standard operating procedures for resolving financial complaints, duplicate transactions, and customer refunds.

2. DISPUTE CATEGORIES & REFUND GUIDELINES
- Double Charge / Duplicate Debit: When a customer is billed twice for the same transaction ID, full refund of the duplicate amount is mandatory.
- Autonomous Approval Threshold: Single dispute refund claims equal to or under ₹25,000 INR (or equivalent) may be automatically processed by the AI Execution Agent if Zero Trust verification passes with >0.85 confidence.
- Mandatory Human Approval Threshold: Single dispute refund claims exceeding ₹25,000 INR (e.g., ₹50,000 INR double charge claims) CANNOT be auto-executed. They MUST be routed to an authorized Operations Executive for Human-in-the-Loop review and approval.

3. PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION (ZERO KNOWLEDGE)
All customer complaints must pass through the Zero Knowledge Privacy Engine. PII including PAN, Aadhaar, Credit Card 16-digit numbers, CVC, passwords, and phone numbers must be redacted prior to external AI processing.

4. AUDIT & FRAUD CONTROL
Every investigation token generates an immutable cryptographically hashed audit trail. Any duplicate dispute submitted within 30 days is automatically flagged for Fraud Analysis.
